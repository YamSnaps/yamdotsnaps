Who am I?

My name is Tayam Abdelfattah, I am an Egyptian American photographer based in New Jersey/New York area. I do concept based portrait photoshoots based on movies, original ideas, where I try to retell or tell a story through images. I work alongside models, photographers, videographers, and honestly any kind of creative to create something new and visually appealing. My main goal is to create cinematic work that gives the viewer the sense that they are watching a movie. I put my all in all my projects on and off the camera to ensure that I create the quality I always aim for.
